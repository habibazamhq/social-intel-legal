Privacy Policy.

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Last updated: 17 October 2026

Short version: Habib Social Intel reads statistics for one TikTok account (@habibazamhq), stores them privately on the operator's own computer, and never sells or shares them. You can revoke access and ask for deletion at any time.

This Policy explains how Habib Social Intel (the "App"), operated by Habib Azam ("we", "us"), handles data obtained through the TikTok Display API.

1. Data we collect

Only after you approve access on TikTok's sign-in page, and only within these scopes:

ScopeData read
user.info.basicOpen ID, display name, avatar
user.info.statsFollower, following, likes and video counts
video.listYour public videos' IDs, titles, creation dates, links, and their view, like, comment and share counts

We also store the OAuth access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, so the App can refresh the data. We do not collect your password, messages, contacts, drafts, private videos, location, or the personal data of viewers or commenters. The App uses no cookies, analytics trackers or advertising on these pages or in the dashboard.

2. How we use it

Solely to show the account owner his own performance analytics: daily snapshots of the counts above, trends over time, and comparisons with his other social channels. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, automated decisions about individuals, or training AI models.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and metrics are stored locally on a private machine controlled by the operator. Tokens sit in a restricted-permission file that is excluded from version control, and metrics are kept in a local database. Data is fetched over encrypted HTTPS connections directly from TikTok. Access is limited to the operator.

4. Retention

Metric snapshots are kept while the App is in use, so that trends can be shown. Tokens are kept only while access is authorised. When access is revoked or the App is discontinued, tokens are deleted, and stored metrics are deleted on request or when no longer needed.

5. Sharing

None. We do not sell, rent, share or disclose TikTok data to any third party. The only exception is where disclosure is required by law, and then only to the extent required.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Revoke access: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (menu names may vary) and remove Habib Social Intel. Data collection stops immediately.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email habibazamhq@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion removes the stored tokens and all metrics for the account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under applicable data-protection law, such as the right to object or to complain to a supervisory authority.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by an adult account owner. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Contact us if you believe we have, and we will delete it.

8. International processing

Data is processed on the operator's private machine, which may be in a different country from you. We apply the protections described in this Policy wherever the data is processed.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes are posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will not apply to previously collected data without your consent where the law requires it.
